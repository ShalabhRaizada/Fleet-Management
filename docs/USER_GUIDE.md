# User Guide — Phase 1 Workflows

Log in at the frontend (default `http://localhost:5173`) using one of the
[seed credentials](../README.md#4-default-login-credentials-from-seed-data).
The sidebar menu is role-based — an Admin sees every module; a Driver or
Workshop Supervisor sees a narrower set.

## Add a vehicle

1. Sidebar → **Vehicles** → **Add Vehicle**.
2. Fill registration number, fuel type (Diesel/CNG/LNG/EV), ownership, branch, status.
3. Save. The vehicle appears in the Vehicle list and on the Fleet Dashboard.
4. Vehicles with status `Breakdown`, `AccidentHold`, `ComplianceHold`,
   `WorkshopHold` or `Inactive` cannot be allocated/coupled — the UI surfaces
   this from the backend's status checks.

## Couple a trailer to a vehicle

1. Sidebar → **Coupling**.
2. Select an available vehicle and an available, compliance-valid trailer.
3. Submit. The backend blocks the action if the trailer is already coupled to
   another vehicle, is unavailable, or has expired compliance.
4. **Decouple** from the same screen; coupling history is kept for audit.

## Log a fuel transaction

1. Sidebar → **Fuel** → **Add Fuel Entry**.
2. Choose fuel type (Diesel/CNG/LNG/EV); the form adjusts units (litres/kg/kWh)
   and EV-specific fields (start/end SoC, charger).
3. Enter odometer reading — the backend rejects odometer values lower than the
   vehicle's last recorded reading unless explicitly overridden.
4. Duplicate entries (same vehicle/date/receipt) are blocked.
5. **Planned vs Actual** view shows variance against `route_fuel_norm`; large
   variances raise an alert and require approval before being finalized.

## Maintain compliance

1. Sidebar → **Compliance** → **Add Compliance Record**.
2. Pick the asset (vehicle/trailer), document type (RC, insurance, fitness, PUC, permit, etc.), issue/expiry dates, upload reference.
3. The **Compliance Expiry** list flags records nearing/at expiry; an expired
   *critical* document places the related asset on `ComplianceHold`,
   blocking trip allocation until renewed or an override is approved.

## Raise and work a job card

1. Sidebar → **Job Cards** → **Create Job Card**, linking a vehicle/trailer,
   workshop, and job type (Scheduled/Breakdown/Accident/Tyre/Accessory/...).
2. Add lines (labour, parts, tyre, consumable, accessory) on the Job Card Detail screen.
3. Update status as work progresses (Open → Assigned → Diagnosis → UnderRepair → QC → Completed → Closed).

## Workshop invoice entry

1. Sidebar → **Workshops** → **Invoices** → **Add Invoice**, linking the job card.
2. An invoice cannot be posted without a job card reference, and duplicate
   vendor invoice numbers are rejected.

## Tyre fitment / removal

1. Sidebar → **Tyres** → select a tyre → **Fit** or **Remove**.
2. Fitment requires the tyre not already be fitted elsewhere (serial-level
   uniqueness is enforced) and captures axle/wheel position.
3. Removal captures odometer and reason.

## Accessories and accompaniments

- **Accessories**: add cameras/ADAS/GPS/OBD/etc. to the accessory master and
  log install/remove/health events against a vehicle.
- **Accompaniments**: issue tarpaulins/seals/OTP locks/safety kits to a trip or
  vehicle from **Accompaniments → Issue**, and record their return from
  **Accompaniments → Return**. Missing mandatory items show up as exceptions
  on the Alert Dashboard.

## Approvals

1. Sidebar → **Approvals** shows your pending approval queue (fuel variance,
   tyre scrap, compliance override, etc., depending on your role).
2. Approve, reject, or send back with mandatory remarks. Every decision is
   recorded in the audit trail.

## Reports and alerts

- **Cost Report**: filterable/exportable vehicle cost ledger.
- **Alert Dashboard**: live list of open exceptions (fuel variance, compliance
  expiring, overdue PM placeholder, tyre rotation due, etc.) generated by the
  alerts engine.
