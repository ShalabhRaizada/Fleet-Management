2. Executive Summary

The Vehicle Maintenance System (VMS) is an integrated fleet management platform designed to manage the complete lifecycle of trucks and trailers from onboarding through daily operations, preventive and corrective maintenance, compliance management, tyre management, and vendor coordination. VMS replaces manual tracking processes with a digitised, role-based, alert-driven system that reduces vehicle downtime, enforces regulatory compliance, and delivers actionable operational intelligence.



2.1  Business Problem

Fleet operations currently suffer from:


Unplanned breakdowns due to missed preventive maintenance schedules.


Manual paper-based tracking of tyres, batteries, GPS devices and accessories.


No centralised repository of vehicle documents (insurance, fitness, permits, PESO certificates).


Absence of expiry alerts for critical compliance documents.


Inability to quickly identify and action non-working vehicles.


No structured process to manage challans, fines, and PUC certificates.



2.2  Objectives



Centralise master data for vendors, vehicles, trailers, and all associated components.


Automate preventive maintenance scheduling and vendor assignment.


Track document expiry and proactively alert stakeholders.


Provide a structured workflow for non-working vehicle repairs.


Enable tyre lifecycle management including cost-per-km analytics.


Support compliance requirements for LNG/CNG tankers (PESO, Piping Certificates).


Deliver standard reports and configurable alerts to management.



3. Scope of the System



3.1  In Scope



User management with role-based access control and login.


Vendor Master (spare parts and service vendors).


Vehicle Master (Trucks) including all identification details and document uploads.


Trailer Master including all identification details and document uploads.


Truck–Trailer coupling and decoupling.


Maintenance Schedule Workbench – per truck and per trailer.


Tyre Maintenance, Tyre Rotation, and Wheel Alignment management.


Battery tracking tagged to each truck.


Additional fittings management (GPS, cameras, telematics, accessories).


Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) and Handover document.


Ongoing preventive maintenance reminders and vendor assignment.


PESO and Piping Certificate tracking with expiry alerts (LNG/CNG tankers).


Non-working vehicle workflow – issue capture, remedial actions, vendor assignment.


Integration with EPIC 1.0 Vehicle Status Report upload.


Challans and fines capture.


PUC certificate management.


Standard reports and alerts.



3.2  Out of Scope



Payroll and driver management.


Fuel management (future phase).


Real-time GPS tracking (data sourced from external GPS devices).


Financial accounting and ERP integration (future phase).

9.2  Decoupling



User selects an active Coupling record and triggers Decouple action.


System captures: Decoupling Date & Time, Decoupled By, Odometer at Decoupling, Remarks.


Both truck and trailer revert to uncoupled status and are available for new couplings.


Full coupling history is retained for audit and maintenance reporting.



9.3  Business Rules



A truck can be coupled to only one trailer at a time.


A trailer can be coupled to only one truck at a time.


Coupling is blocked if either vehicle has &apos;Non-Working&apos; or &apos;Scrapped&apos; status.


Coupling history is immutable; corrections require a new transaction with remarks.

10.3  Reminder and Assignment Workflow



System generates a reminder to the Maintenance Manager when a PM event is within the configured alert window (default: 7 days or 500 km before due).


Maintenance Manager reviews the reminder notification, opens the Workbench, and assigns the job to a Vendor.


Vendor receives a notification (email / SMS / in-app).


Workshop Supervisor updates job progress and completion.


On completion, next PM event is auto-scheduled.


Overdue jobs escalate to Fleet Manager after a configurable grace period (default: 2 days).
